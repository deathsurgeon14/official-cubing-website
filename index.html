Deccan Cubing

Deccan Cubing is a premier, non-profit community organization founded in 2017 that is dedicated to cultivating speedcubing across Hyderabad and South India. Operating as the regional cornerstone for the speedcubing community, the organization bridges passion and professional mind sports, using the traditional household puzzle to foster cognitive development, concentration, and spatial awareness among youth.
