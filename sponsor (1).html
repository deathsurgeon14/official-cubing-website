OUR SPONSOR

Deccan Cubing

The Go-To Speedcubing Organization

About Deccan Cubing

  • Is a premier, non-profit community organization founded in 2017 dedicated to developing and cultivating the sport of speedcubing across Hyderabad and South India.
  • Transformed a traditional household puzzle into an analytical, high-octane competitive avenue.

What They Do

  • In strategic alliance with the WCA and the Indian Cube Federation
  • Hosts globally recognized, official speedcubing tournaments throughout the year.
  • Events routinely draw hundreds of participants and thousands of spectators.

About The Founder

  • Pioneered by Pavan Kumar Akula, a world-renowned speedcuber whose credentials include holding >
    • A World No. 1 ranking in the 3x3 blindfolded category (2013)
    • Several Guinness, Asian, and Limca world records
  • Established Deccan Cubing to democratize puzzle solving, alongside his brother Sai Kumar

How They Are Helping Us

  • Supplying official timers across all eight competition stations.
  • Sharing hands-on organizing experience and event coordination skills.
  • Ensuring the main competition floor runs efficiently and without delays.
